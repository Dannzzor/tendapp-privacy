# Tend Journal — Support

Need help or have feedback? We'd love to hear from you.

## Contact

Email us at [tend.app.support.us@gmail.com](mailto:tend.app.support.us@gmail.com)

## Common Questions

**Where is my data stored?**
All your journal entries are stored locally on your device. We never have access to them.

**How do I export my data?**
Go to Settings → Export everything. This creates a JSON file you can save or share.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Open the Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → Tend Premium → Cancel.

**I lost my entries after deleting the app.**
Because Tend stores everything on-device for privacy, deleting the app removes your data. We recommend using the export feature regularly as a backup.

**What is "Go Deeper"?**
An optional feature that sends a single entry to Claude (by Anthropic) for deeper reflection questions. Your name is stripped and nothing is stored. It requires a premium subscription for unlimited use.
